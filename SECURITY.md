# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email **security@lykoilabs.dev**. Do not open a public issue for
anything that could be a security problem.

Include what you can of:

- the repository and version or commit affected,
- a description of the issue and its impact,
- steps or a proof of concept to reproduce it,
- whether you want to be credited, and under what name.

You will get an acknowledgement within **5 working days**. We will keep you
informed as we confirm the issue, work on a fix and plan disclosure. We ask
for a reasonable embargo (typically up to 90 days) while a fix is prepared;
we will not take legal action against good-faith research that respects
this policy and avoids privacy violations, data destruction and service
disruption.

## Scope

All repositories under the `lykoilabs` organisation and the services under
`lykoilabs.dev`. Game servers built *on* Tenshu by third parties are out of
scope; report those to their operators.

## Supported versions

Tenshu is pre-1.0. Only the latest release of each crate and the `main`
branch receive fixes.

## Encryption

If you need to send sensitive material encrypted, ask for a current key at
the address above before sending.
